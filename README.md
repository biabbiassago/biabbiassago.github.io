Hello! I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Statistics at the University of California, Irvine, where I am advised by Veronica J. Berrocal.

My research interests are in spatial and spatiotemporal statistics, with a particular focus on statistical inference when data are collected through non-representative or preferential sampling. My dissertation focuses on developing spatial models and methods that account for preferential sampling in different settings, including applications to extreme environmental pollution.

I am also interested in sampling and study design, particularly the statistical implications of how data are (badly) collected. I enjoy collaborating on applied projects and learning from scientists with expertise in environmental science, earth science, and epidemiology.
